Personal Profile

Belal Uddin — known as Solas Belal

Solas Belal is the public creator identity of Belal Uddin, a Bangladeshi digital content creator.

He was born on 15 December 2006 in Naikhongchhari, Bandarban, Bangladesh. His public hometown is Ramu, Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh.

Through social media, he has developed a personality-driven creator identity centred on direct expression, attitude-based presentation, social commentary, entertainment and creator lifestyle content.
