Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or interact with the official Solas Belal website.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the official Solas Belal website available at solasbelal.github.io/solas-belal/.

The website is primarily an informational creator website providing biography, media, gallery, social profile and contact information related to Solas Belal.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information You Provide

The website does not require visitors to create an account or submit personal information in order to browse its public pages.

If you choose to contact Solas Belal by email, the information you voluntarily include in that communication may be received and processed. This may include your name, email address, organisation, message content and any other information you choose to provide.

3. How Contact Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily provided through email may be used only as reasonably necessary to understand, manage and respond to your inquiry.

Examples may include responding to business inquiries, creator collaboration proposals, media communication or other legitimate professional requests.

Sending an email does not guarantee a response, acceptance of a proposal or establishment of any business relationship.

4. Automatically Processed Technical Information

This website is hosted using third-party web infrastructure. As with most websites, hosting providers and network services may automatically process certain technical information required to deliver the website.

Such information may include IP address, browser type, device information, request time, referring information or similar technical data. The handling of infrastructure-level information may be governed by the policies of the relevant hosting or network provider.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

The core public website does not require visitors to create accounts or use authentication cookies in order to browse its content.

Browser technologies, hosting services or future website features may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies when technically necessary or when additional functionality is introduced.

If the website later introduces analytics, embedded services or other features that materially change data collection practices, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

6. External Social Media Links

The website contains links to official Solas Belal profiles on third-party platforms including TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Threads.

When you follow an external link, you leave this website and become subject to the privacy policy, terms and data practices of that third-party platform.

Solas Belal does not control how third-party platforms collect, process or store information about their users.

7. External Websites

The website may contain links to external websites or online services for reference, communication or social networking purposes.

The inclusion of an external link does not mean that this website controls or accepts responsibility for the external service's privacy practices, security or content.

Visitors should review the relevant privacy policies before providing personal information to any external service.

8. Personal Information Not Published

The website is designed to separate public creator information from private personal information.

Exact residential addresses, private phone numbers and other unnecessary private details are not intentionally published as part of the public creator profile.

Location references on biography or profile pages are limited to appropriate public-facing geographic information.

9. Data Sharing

Personal information voluntarily sent through professional communication is not intended to be sold to third parties.

Information may, however, be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect legal rights, address security concerns or respond to a valid legal process.

10. Data Retention

Information received through direct communication may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the relevant inquiry, maintain business records, protect legitimate interests or meet applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably needed may be deleted when appropriate.

11. Data Security

Reasonable care is taken when handling public website information and direct professional communications.

However, no website, email service, network transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Visitors should avoid sending highly sensitive, confidential or unnecessary personal information through ordinary email.

12. Children's Privacy

This website is a general public creator website and is not designed as a service for collecting personal information from children.

Visitors should not submit another person's sensitive or private information without appropriate authority or permission.

13. Your Choices

You may choose not to send personal information through email or external platforms.

You can browse the primary public content of this website without creating an account.

Where applicable, requests concerning personal information previously provided through direct communication may be submitted using the official contact email.

14. Third-Party Services

This website may depend on third-party services for hosting, domain-related infrastructure, browsers, social platforms or other technical functionality.

Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their practices are outside the direct control of this website.

15. International Access

The website can be accessed from different countries and regions. Internet infrastructure and third-party services may process information in locations outside the visitor's country.

Visitors are responsible for understanding any privacy requirements that may apply to their use of third-party platforms linked from this site.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised when website functionality, legal requirements or information handling practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date where appropriate.

Continued use of the website after an updated policy is published means that the current version will apply to subsequent use.

17. Contact

Questions relating to this Privacy Policy or public website privacy may be sent to:

Solas Belal
Email: solasbelalnc@gmail.com

For general business or creator communication, please use the official contact page.
